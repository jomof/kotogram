# Planned Grammar Points

Candidates identified during false-positive mining. Each line: `title|nuance`.

だけでなく〜も (not only… but also)|The standard N3 pattern X だけでなく Y も, indicating addition beyond X; distinct from はもちろん (inclusive emphasis) and のみならず (formal/literary)
ばかりか〜も (not only… but also)|Emphatic variant emphasizing surprise that even Y is true in addition to X; stronger emotional charge than だけでなく
ともなれば (once it reaches the level of)|Conditional variant of ともなると; emphasizes that upon reaching a significant status/situation, a natural consequence follows
にもなれば (once one reaches)|Marks reaching a milestone age, rank, or stage where something naturally becomes true; overlaps with ともなれば but attaches to specific quantities or statuses
どれだけ〜ても (no matter how much)|Interrogative-based concessive; functionally similar to いくら〜でも and どんなに〜ても but uses どれだけ to emphasize the unknown/unlimited degree
好きなだけ (as much as one wants)|Expresses freedom to do something without limit; 好きなだけ + Verb means "do as much as you like"; distinct from できるだけ (as much as possible, implying effort)
これから (from now on)|Temporal demonstrative indicating future direction or intention; これから + Verb = "from now / going forward"; distinct from plain これ (proximal demonstrative)
これより (hereby / from this point)|Formal set phrase used in announcements and official proceedings; これより開催いたします = "we hereby begin"; distinct from comparative より
時間・費用がかかる (to take time / to cost)|Expresses time or money required for an action; Verb-のに + 時間がかかる; one of the most common かかる senses, distinct from にかかっている (to depend on)
病気にかかる (to catch a disease)|Expresses contracting an illness; 風邪にかかる, インフルエンザにかかる; distinct from にかかっている (depend on) and 時間がかかる (take time)
電話がかかってくる (to receive a phone call)|A phone call arrives/comes in; distinct from other かかる senses; very common in everyday Japanese
Noun より Noun (comparative)|Basic comparative particle marking the standard of comparison; X より Y のほうが = "Y is more ~ than X"; distinct from 何より (more than anything)
何が何でも (no matter what / by all means)|Emphatic determination; expresses unwavering resolve to accomplish something regardless of obstacles; distinct from 何でも (anything)
何なりと (anything at all / whatever you wish)|Polite invitation to request freely; commonly 何なりとお申し付けください; distinct from 何でも (anything) by its deferential register
により・による (due to / by means of)|Formal causal or agentive marker; 事故により遅延 = "delayed due to accident"; distinct from comparative より and the existing に至って/によるものだ patterns
医者にかかる (to see a doctor)|Set expression; かかる in the sense of receiving professional services; distinct from にかかっている (depend on) and 病気にかかる (catch illness)
鍵がかかる (to be locked)|Physical state expression; かかる in the sense of a lock being engaged; distinct from other かかる patterns
よりによって (of all things / of all times)|Expresses dismay or irony about an unfortunate coincidence; よりによって今日 = "of all days, today"; distinct from comparative より and 何より
遠慮なく (without hesitation / feel free)|Adverbial set phrase inviting someone to act freely; 遠慮なくどうぞ; distinct from なくても (even if not) and なくて (causal negative)
お構いなく (don't bother / don't mind me)|Polite set phrase declining attention or hospitality; どうぞお構いなく; distinct from なくても (even if not) and なくて (causal negative)
Sentence-final の (casual explanation・question)|Casual の at end of sentences for explanation (美味しいの。) or confirmation-seeking (本当なの？); the informal face of 〜のだ/〜んだ without the copula; cross-cuts all grammar levels as a register marker [KC discovery]
Question word + の (casual interrogative)|どうしたの？ どこにいるの？ なぜ〜の？ — Casual question pattern combining interrogatives (どう/どこ/なぜ/どうやって) with sentence-final の？; extremely frequent in spoken Japanese; distinct from formal question constructions [KC discovery]
俺・僕 register (informal masculine speech)|Cross-cutting masculine speech pattern using first-person 俺/僕 with casual copula (だ/だよ/だぞ/だろ); marks informal male register as a pragmatic layer distinct from individual grammar points [KC discovery]
お前 + command forms (rough masculine directives)|Rough masculine register combining お前/お前ら with imperative forms (せよ/よ/ろ) and blunt assertions; stronger than standard imperative; a register pattern rather than a single construction [KC discovery]
わ (masculine usage)|Sentence-ending particle used by men to express sudden realization or mild exasperation
